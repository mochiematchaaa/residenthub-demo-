Title defense walkthrough (about 5 minutes). Open the resident demo and the admin demo in two tabs; they update each other.
  1. Resident, Book a facility: pick a date on the calendar, pick a time, send the request. It shows as Pending.
  2. Admin, Facility: approve the request. The resident sees Approved under Reservations.
  3. Resident, Dues: choose a charge, pick how you paid, type any reference number and send the payment details.
  4. Admin, Finance: find the payment from Unit 3B under Payments to verify and confirm it. A receipt number is issued and the due shows as paid.
  5. Resident, Parking: pick a free slot, choose a monthly or daily rental and pay it. The admin confirms that payment under Finance and the slot is assigned.
  6. Admin, Announcements and Polls: post one. The resident reads it and votes.
  7. Admin, Reports and Activity log: everything that was just done is recorded.
